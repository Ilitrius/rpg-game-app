New
• Realistic graphics for areas 6 to 100: all 19 landscapes (Heath, Desert, Volcano, Crypt, Frost, Jungle, Ruins, Glow-Mushroom Caves, Coast, Bone Desert, Blood Garden, Sky, Underworld, Shadow, Clockwork, Dream, Astral, Void and End) have new, detailed backdrops with sky, celestial bodies, distant views and a fitting ground.
• Realistic monsters in these areas: slimes, hounds, insects, golems, humanoids, winged creatures, serpents and wraiths, each as enemy and as boss, in the colors of their area with glowing eyes.
• Realistic weapon in hand: dagger, short sword, mace, axe and the bare fist now appear in combat as detailed graphics with a leather glove and arm bracer.
• The weapon in the Character tab and on the hero figure in Equipment now looks realistic too.

Note
• Areas 2 to 5 (Forest, Swamp, Mountains, Cave) will get the realistic graphics in a later version.
