New
• Realistic graphics in every area: all 100 areas have new, detailed backdrops with sky, celestial bodies, distant views and a fitting ground (Forest, Swamp, Mountains, Cave and the 19 other landscapes from Heath to End).
• Realistic monsters everywhere: Meadow, Forest, Swamp, Mountains and Cave have their own enemies and bosses (for example wolf, spider, mushroom, toad, will-o'-the-wisp, bat, treant, hydra, yeti and cave dragon). Areas 6 to 100 show slimes, hounds, insects, golems, humanoids, winged creatures, serpents and wraiths in the colors of their area with glowing eyes.
• Realistic weapon in hand: dagger, short sword, mace, axe and the bare fist now appear in combat as detailed graphics with a leather glove and arm bracer.
• The weapon in the Character tab and on the hero figure in Equipment now looks realistic too.
