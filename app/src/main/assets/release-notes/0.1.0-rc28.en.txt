New
• New: achievements with Google Play Games. Sign in under Options → Game account; 10 achievements (kills, level, bosses, areas, gold) are then unlocked.
• New: ranks for your hero, the end-game system! From the end level 999 you buy up to 15 ranks with gold in the Character tab (Squire to Demigod, then Mythos I-V, which also need rebirths). Every rank makes you permanently stronger: more damage, health and speed, and from Knight on more gold and inventory slots. The bonuses apply from level 999 and are kept after a rebirth.
• New: rank badges as realistic shields in bronze, silver, gold and violet, with gems for Mythos.
• New goals: "Buy ranks" (from the end level).
• Realistic graphics in all 100 areas: Swamp, Mountains and Cave have new monsters and landscapes, and all 19 biomes after them have their own detailed backdrops.
• The monsters of the later areas were redesigned (eight body shapes from slime to dragon) and wear the colours of their biome. Bosses and especially dangerous foes look more menacing, with spikes, horns and glowing cracks.
• Weapons in hand: the arm, glove, fist and every weapon in the first-person view are now realistic. On the Short Sword, Mace and Axe the gem shows the rarity.
• New Forest backdrop with trees, light and moss.
• New Leather Gloves and Gauntlets with stitching, lames and rivets; without a weapon the Character tab shows a realistic fist.
• Elixirs in the shop are real bottles with see-through glass and glowing liquid.
• Your weapon in the Character tab and on the equipment figure is now realistic too.

Improved
• Gold and loot are better balanced: monsters give half the gold, items drop less often but are at least uncommon and more often rare or better. Offline you get at most 10 items.
• The Boar is now a wild boar with bristly fur, a bristle crest, tusks and glowing eyes.
• The tutorial explains more: goals, rebirth, equipment, shop and elixirs, auto-boss, endless tiers and offline progress (9 steps).
• Leather Cap, Leather Vest, Leather and Plate Boots are more detailed: stitching, grain, straps and plates.
• Blades shine evenly without stripes; gems sparkle from Rare upwards.
• Monsters and backdrops load in the background so combat stays smooth when you change areas.
• The buff icons in combat now show the elixir bottles.
• The white hit flash on monsters is subtler and shorter, so bright monsters no longer look washed out during fast attacks.
• The privacy summary in Options now mentions the optional Google Play Games sign-in and what it transmits.

Fixed
• After a boss victory the hero reliably travels on to the next zone, even when saving takes a little longer.
