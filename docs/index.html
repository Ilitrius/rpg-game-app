Idle RPG – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Summary: “Idle RPG” (com.rp.IDLEGAMERP) does not collect, transmit to any server, or share any personal data.

Contact

Ilitrius (René P.), email: rene.puf@gmail.com
