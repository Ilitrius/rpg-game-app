## English

**Title (30):** `Idle RPG: Monster Hunt` (22) – or keep `Rp Idle Combat Game`

**Short description (80):**
`First-person idle RPG: defeat monsters, collect loot and level up your hero.` (76)

**Description:**

```
Your hero roams the land and fights all on their own. You decide how they grow stronger.

FIRST-PERSON IDLE RPG
See every fight through your hero's eyes. Monsters appear in front of you, your weapon strikes, the loot drops. You don't have to tap anything to make progress. Watch your hero fight, or jump in yourself: every tap on a monster is an extra hit, and quick streaks build up a combo.

YOUR HERO, YOUR CHOICE
Every victory grants experience. With each level up you freely spend stat points on Strength, Dexterity, Vitality and Luck. Your choices decide how hard your hero hits, how fast they attack, how much they can take and how often they land critical hits. Will you build a tank, a fast striker or a lucky charm?

100 ZONES FULL OF MONSTERS
Start in the meadow and fight your way through forest, swamp and mountains into the cave. Then the real journey begins: thorny heaths, sand seas, ash lands, frozen wastes, poison jungles, sunken ruins, a realm of shadows, a clockwork fortress and many more, all the way to the final throne. Every zone has its own monsters and a boss. Defeat it and your hero moves on to the next zone. If you like, bosses are challenged automatically.

LOOT AND GEAR
Monsters drop gold and equipment. Items come in five rarities, from common to legendary. Equip the best pieces, compare stats right in the inventory and sell what you don't need, one by one or all at once. Your inventory holds 40 items. When it's full, extra loot is turned into gold automatically.

THE SHOP
Spend your gold on new weapons and armor. The stock changes regularly and grows with your hero. You can also restock it for gold when you're hunting for an upgrade.

GOALS, DAILY REWARD AND STATISTICS
Complete goals for bonus gold, claim your daily reward and build a streak. Statistics show your biggest hit, the gold you've earned and your play time.

PROGRESS EVEN WHEN YOU'RE AWAY
Your hero keeps fighting when you close the app. A reward awaits you when you return (up to 8 hours). Offline earnings are lower than active play, so playing actively pays off.

BACK UP YOUR SAVE
Export your save as a file and import it again, for example when you switch devices.

GRAPHICS AND SOUND
High-resolution vector graphics that stay sharp on modern displays, plus sound effects with adjustable volume. Playable in English and German.

PRIVACY
• Your save stays on your device
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• No internet connection required

HIGHLIGHTS
• First-person idle combat with taps and combos
• Free stat point distribution
• 100 zones with their own monsters and bosses
• Loot in five rarities
• Shop with rotating stock
• Goals, daily reward and statistics
• Offline progress up to 8 hours
• Save export and import

The game has no purchases. If you enjoy it, you can make a voluntary donation in the options. It unlocks nothing in the game.

Start now, and your hero sets out on their journey.
```
